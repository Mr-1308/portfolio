02 / How I work

Connecting engineering decisions

From site constraints to design

Assess terrain, environmental and permitting constraints; develop layouts; compare production and technical alternatives; and coordinate electrical assumptions with the wider project team.

Grid and operational requirements

Work with export limits, power factor and reactive-power requirements, RfG considerations and PV+BESS operating assumptions. These are evaluated per project and jurisdiction, not treated as universal values.

Cross-disciplinary coordination

Align Civil, Electrical, Grid, Project Development and Procurement inputs around access, drainage, cable routes, transformer locations, supplier specifications and technical documentation.

Practical construction awareness

On-site installation experience informs attention to routing, equipment access, installation sequence and the gap between drawings and field conditions.
